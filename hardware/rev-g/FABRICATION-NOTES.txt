geometry. Solid thermal-plane connections are intentional; do not replace
these with thermal-relief spokes. This revision replaces all G.4 tenting,
mask-cap-survival and exact-stencil instructions.

STANDARD PCBA, TOP ONLY: fit exactly U1/U2/U4/U5/U6/L1 per matching BOM/CPL.

layer is reference only, NOT an exact-aperture or thickness requirement.
No step stencil, custom stencil file or paste on unpopulated pads requested.
Solder U1's exposed pad; control U2 paste to avoid lifting its outer leads.
Use normal assembly inspection; no custom inspection report requested.

Add necessary temporary assembly rails/fiducials externally, preserving the
