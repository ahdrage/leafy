# Rev F final recheck — 12 September 2026

See [DESIGN-REVIEW.md](DESIGN-REVIEW.md) for the reviewed substitutions, native and independent checks, and the physical tests still required. U3 pin 5 is now **SHDN, grounded**. All other circuit nets are retained.

Use the Rev F board and Rev F hand BOM together. The old TCAN3403DRQ1 is not the correct U3 for this revision. Factory assembly remains U1/U2/U4/L1 only.

The [Rev E review](../rev-e/RECHECK-2026-09-12.md) is preserved as historical evidence; it describes the previous CAN chip and is not the Rev F purchasing authority.
