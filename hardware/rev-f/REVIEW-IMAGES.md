# Rev F review images

The images in `exports/` are regenerated from Rev F native CAD. Share `deliverables/leaf-heat-rev-f-review-images.zip` for a compact review set.

- `board-3d.png`: board overview; generic DB9 model is approximate and F1 lacks a body model.
- `top.png`, `ground.png`, `power.png`, `bottom.png`: copper and plane views.
- `can-change.png`: close view of U3 pin 5 and the retained C13 bypass.
- `assembly-map.png`: orange factory positions; remaining positions hand-soldered.
- `back-legend.png`: printed hand-part reference table.
- `schematic.png` and `schematic.pdf`: updated circuit and pin-5 grounding.

The images are engineering review aids, not proof of working hardware or exact enclosure fit.
