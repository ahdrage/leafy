# Rev E electrical recheck — 12 September 2026

**Result: the static wiring checks pass. One purchasing-code problem and two instruction problems were corrected. This remains an untested prototype, with no runnable heater firmware.** These findings do not establish that the finished board heats this particular car or tolerates its electrical environment.

## Corrections made

1. **D1 purchasing code:** the inherited `SS110-13-F` entry could not be verified against the named manufacturer's documentation. Replaced it with **Diodes Incorporated B1100-13-F**, documented as a 100 V, 1 A Schottky in SMA. Updated the schematic, PCB purchasing/value fields, back legend, BOM and packages. Its cathode still connects to VPWR and its anode to CAR_FUSED. Existing pads are 2.5 × 1.8 mm, 4.0 mm centre spacing and 1.5 mm gap; these accommodate the manufacturer's suggested 2.5 × 1.7 mm SMA pattern. No pads, routes or circuit nets changed. [Manufacturer datasheet](https://www.diodes.com/datasheet/download/B1100.pdf).
2. **Connector assembly:** PROGRAMMING.md incorrectly said the manufacturer should solder J1/J3. Both belong to the 37 hand-fitted parts; the instruction now agrees with the actual factory BOM.
3. **First-power limits:** the old 3.22–3.41 V interval represented reference/resistor tolerances only. It omitted the regulator's light-load PFM allowance. The hand guide now explains the estimated 3.446 V upper value and requires measuring startup/ripple/load behaviour within the downstream 3.0–3.6 V range. This is not a new regulator setting. [TI regulator datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lmr36510.pdf), [ESP32 module datasheet](https://www.espressif.com/sites/default/files/documentation/esp32-c3-wroom-02_datasheet_en.pdf).

Added [VEHICLE-WIRING.md](VEHICLE-WIRING.md) because the earlier development-board instructions do not apply to Rev E GPIOs or its fixed CAN connector pair.

## Specific matters still requiring resolution or measurement

- **Exact car configuration:** Rev E is wired to the earlier EV-CAN pair. Confirm the model-year/TCU arrangement before transmitting. A later configuration requires a reviewed cable or hardware change as well as different commands. The supplied registration alone cannot settle this. Cable mapping and installation references are in [VEHICLE-WIRING.md](VEHICLE-WIRING.md).
- **Input hot-plug behaviour:** there is no damped bulk input capacitor. TI specifically discusses cable inductance resonating with ceramic input capacitors and recommends a 20–100 µF capacitor with suitable ESR for damping when needed. Check VPWR at the regulator using the actual cable and controlled supply. Add and validate damping if ringing is excessive; a TVS does not establish that this test passes. [TI supply recommendations, section 8.4](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lmr36510.pdf).
- **Parked battery use:** the board has no hardware low-voltage disconnect. Its ADC measures after D1, so firmware must calibrate the diode drop and choose sleep/low-battery behaviour. Measure the actual 12 V input current with Wi-Fi connected, unavailable and reconnecting. Keeping the radio reachable affects the battery budget.
- **Factory soldering:** U1/U2 have ordinary plated thermal holes, not filled/capped vias. The assembler must resolve solder wicking, paste coverage and inspection. Factory placement coordinates are KiCad origins; check the vendor's actual package alignment/rotation preview.
- **Physical environment:** supply ripple, thermal behaviour, vibration, transients, EMI and cold operation remain untested. The buttons are rated only −25 to +70 °C and cannot be washed. The DB9 housing and antenna overhang the board; the approximate 3D model is not an enclosure drawing. [Omron button datasheet](https://components.omron.com/sites/default/files/datasheet_pdf/A070-E1.pdf).

The 65 V regulator specification is **not** a 65 V continuous-input specification for the complete board. No common-mode chokes were added. There is no USB data pair on this revision; J3 is a UART service connector.

## Bring-up order

1. Confirm factory placement and exposed-pad soldering. Hand-fit and inspect all remaining components, then check polarity and shorts with power disconnected.
2. Follow [HAND-ASSEMBLY.md](HAND-ASSEMBLY.md) using a current-limited 12 V bench supply, initially holding ESP_EN low. Measure the input, protected input and 3.3 V rail, including startup and load changes. Test with the intended cable before car use.
3. Build minimal firmware for the GPIO map in [VEHICLE-WIRING.md](VEHICLE-WIRING.md). Prove UART recovery, Wi-Fi and CAN on a properly terminated bench network. Verify that reset, flashing and reconnection do not transmit unwanted commands.
4. Confirm the car's cable/TCU configuration and prove receive-only operation before a stationary On/Off test. Verify actual climate behaviour, including Off during an unfinished On sequence.
5. Measure overnight/extended parked current, loss of Wi-Fi and low-battery handling before leaving the device installed unattended.

The CAD review is complete for this pass. The physical tests above have not been performed, and no order or production release was made.
