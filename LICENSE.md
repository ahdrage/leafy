# Licence scope

The licence choice for original Leafy firmware, scripts, documentation and hardware is not yet finalized. Public availability alone does not grant a general permission to reuse that original material. This statement does not restrict any rights already granted by the upstream licences below.

## Existing third-party material

- The OVMS-derived Leaf protocol retains its original MIT copyright and permission notice in [OVMS-NOTICE.txt](firmware/leaf-heat/OVMS-NOTICE.txt).
- KiCad-derived library symbols, footprints and model material retain the [KiCad library licence and design exception](LICENSES/KiCad-Libraries.md), with the [CC-BY-SA-4.0 text](LICENSES/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt). The design exception is distinct from redistributing library collections.
- ESP-IDF, PlatformIO, KiCad, Blender, Freerouting and other external dependencies retain their own licences. Toolchains are not vendored here.
- Manufacturer names, drawings, datasheets and trademarks are not relicensed as Leafy-owned material. Manufacturer documentation is linked at source.

The CERN-OHL-P-2.0 text in `LICENSES/` is included for the proposed hardware licence selection; its presence alone does not yet apply it to the original project. Project licence selection will be recorded explicitly here when decided.
